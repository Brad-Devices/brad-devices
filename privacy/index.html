Privacy Policy

The short version: we collect your email address only if you give it to us, we do not sell it, we do not advertise to you, and there are no tracking cookies on this site. Everything else is detail.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form. There is no other collection.

WhereWhatWhy
Follow the buildEmail address, and the segment you pick (software, silicon, or press)To email you when something actually ships in a segment you asked about
Press inquiryEmail, outlet, deadline, and your angleTo answer your inquiry before your deadline
Everything elseNothingNo accounts, no cookies set by us, no analytics, no tracking pixels

No tracking, and we can prove it

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Every page is a static file you can read the source of. If a future version adds measurement of any kind, this page changes first — that is the whole point of publishing it.

Who holds it

A third-party list service, not us.

Announcement subscriptions are held by Buttondown, a mailing-list service, under double opt-in — you confirm by email that you asked to be added, which is also what filters automated submissions. Brad Devices does not keep its own copy of the subscriber list.

Your address is never committed to a public repository

This project's source is published on GitHub and mirrored to a removable SD card for verification. Subscriber data is never written into either. That is a deliberate rule, not an oversight — see the waitlist policy in COMPANY/POLICIES.md.

Press inquiries go by email to the address below and are handled as one-off correspondence, not added to any announcement list unless you ask.

Why we use it, and on what basis

Consent, and nothing else.

The lawful basis for holding your address is consent, given when you submit the form and confirmed when you click the opt-in link. You may withdraw it at any time, and withdrawing it stops every email immediately.

We do not use your address for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making. We do not sell or rent it. There are no third-party advertising or analytics trackers on this site.

Your rights

You can ask for any of this, and it will be done.

RightWhat that means here
AccessWe will confirm whether we hold your address and which segment it is on
CorrectionWe will fix or update a wrong address or name
DeletionWe will remove you from the service entirely, on request — not just stop emailing you
UnsubscribeEvery email contains a one-click unsubscribe link. Using it withdraws consent
Object / restrictAsk us to stop processing while we resolve it

Deletion requests go to brad.devices.official@gmail.com and are actioned within 30 days. This policy follows Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

How long we keep it

Until you leave, or we close the list.

Your subscription is held until you unsubscribe or ask to be deleted, at which point it is removed from the list service. A press inquiry is kept only as long as needed to answer it and any agreed follow-up.

Children

This announcement list is not directed at children. Brad Devices is founded by a 15-year-old, but the list collects contact details from adults who can consent for themselves, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has signed up, write to us and it will be removed.

Contact

brad.devices.official@gmail.com

Privacy questions, access requests and deletion requests all go to the same address. A human reads them — Brad Devices is one person and a small team, so expect a reply from us rather than a ticketing system.
