FOUNDER · THE RECORD

Where it started

21 essays in fifteen weeks across 2025, then eleven months of silence, then one essay that came back with checksums in it. I am not publishing them again and I am not defending them. They are here because they are the record of how this started, in the order it happened, including the parts I would write differently.

Read in order and you can watch a brand get invented, get renamed twice, get retired into its own parent, and then get rebuilt as a company that ships. The argument moves from feeling to declaration to heat, gets corrected on 10 October, turns sharp again four days later, and then stops in November — because for eleven months I had nothing to point at. That silence is the most honest thing on this page, and the twenty-second essay exists because of it.

Three things are true here now that were not true when this page was first built. The earliest writing is from August 2025 and belongs to a company called BradTech Industries that no longer exists. Brad Devices appears in its own title on 7 September, a month before Measured Fire — which is the essay usually credited with naming it, and was credited with it here until this record was found. And the piece this archive long called a broken reference has its parent sitting two cards above it.

Nothing here is edited. A record is not a document, and the two essays that were wrong are still standing exactly as written — because the correction went into a new essay, thirty-eight days later, and still does.

The compiler receipt

#braddevices #compilers

I'm building Brad Devices, a computer company in Kisumu, Kenya. The rule is
software before silicon, receipts after that.

The receipt I want to show first is a compiler.

It compiles a C subset to BradISA V2, my own 32-bit instruction set. A small
emulator then runs the emitted code inside the test suite. Not "it assembles."
It executes.

add(3, 4) returns 7.
sum(10) returns 45.
An eight-argument function returns 36.
All 24 tests are green.

The language is deliberately small: int and void, functions, locals, if/else,
while, globals, arrays, pointers, int-field structs. No optimizer, no C++. It
exists to prove the ISA can be compiled at all — the part that was honestly
missing.
