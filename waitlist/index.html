What you will never get from us

We cannot take a deposit, a pre-order, or a reservation fee, because there is no product and no foundry. We will not ask for one until that stops being true.

A benchmark we haven't measured

Every number we publish comes with the file that produced it. We have turned down comparisons we couldn't reproduce.
