The profiles, as the platforms return them

This page is the record of where Brad Devices can be read. Nothing here is typed from memory: the profiles are fetched from the platforms, and the bio and counts are the platforms' own.

The four profiles are not equal. Three can be read from outside and are shown exactly as the platforms return them — the real avatar, the real bio, the real counts. LinkedIn serves a login wall to everyone who is not already signed in, so its profile is listed without a picture and says why. Its individual post pages are readable, which is why the first post is recorded in the journal and the profile is only listed here.
